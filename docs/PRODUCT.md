# Jobyssey

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

A web app in the browser that the Job Seeker runs on their own computer, using their own LLM Provider or Harness. No mobile app in v1.

## Users

The primary user is a Job Seeker, whether currently employed or unemployed.

## Product Purpose

Jobyssey is a platform dedicated to helping people find a job.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Finds Job Postings from Job Sources in any country or region the Job Seeker picks.
- AI work runs as Modes with fixed instructions, tools and result shapes, so results are predictable and token use stays low.
- The stack is decided; initial workflows remain open.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Jobyssey.
