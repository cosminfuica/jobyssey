# Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-ALv2)

The codebase is published under the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-ALv2), a Fair Source license: anyone may read, run, modify and self-host Jobyssey, but may not offer it as a competing commercial service, and each release converts to Apache 2.0 two years after it ships.

## Considered Options

- **AGPL-3.0**: OSI open source and keeps hosted forks open, but does not stop others from selling a hosted Jobyssey.
- **MIT**: maximum adoption, no protection against a competing hosted service.

## Consequences

- FSL is not OSI "open source": describe Jobyssey as source-available or Fair Source.
- Outside contributions are open from day one. Contributors sign a contributor license agreement once, through cla-assistant.io, so the license can still change later.
